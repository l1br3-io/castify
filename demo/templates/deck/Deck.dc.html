Principles

Working rules

  • Hairline rules carry the structure — never boxes or heavy fills.
  • Two faces organise the page: Cormorant Garamond display over Lora text.
  • Gold is drawn as stroke — borders, rules and underlines, never filled blocks.

Two columns

Justified, as the book sets

Body copy justifies at a comfortable measure, with automatic hyphenation keeping the word-spaces even — the editorial setting this whole system is built around.

A hairline between

A single hairline parts the columns at the gutter’s center, the column rule of a set page. Both sides share the baseline rhythm, line for line across the gutter.

Three columns

Narrower

Three columns tighten the measure to about thirty characters; the justified block holds, but the hyphens work harder — keep the copy brief.

Figures align

Numbers set tabular wherever they stand as figures, so a column of values reads straight down the page.

Quiet feet

The spacing scale is airy by design; short paragraphs and open column feet keep the page composed.

Quadrants

Sonnet

Demanding, brief — every word earns its seat.

Monograph

Demanding, long — the argument needs its chapters.

Letter

Light, brief — read once, kept forever.

Serial novel

Light, long — the turned pages do the carrying.

03

Imagery

The plate treatment

Every photograph sits matted on the page — the plate wrapper grades it warm and archival inside a surface-colored mat with a hairline outline, like a tipped-in book plate.

Full bleed

One photograph, edge to edge — the scrim is the paper token rising from the foot, and the mat stays behind: a plate sits on the page, a bleed is the page.

Half bleed

  • The photograph bleeds to three edges and holds half the stage
  • The copy keeps the content rhythm — same gridlines as every slide
  • Bullets still hang left of the text, whatever sits behind them

Mirrored

The flip class changes the photograph’s side and the copy returns to the text axis — one pattern, both directions.
